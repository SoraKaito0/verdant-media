About Verdant

Verdant is a VRChat world interface and community system featuring interactive menus, media controls, personalisation, supporter recognition and world management tools.

Development builds and installation packages are distributed separately and are not offered on this page.

Community content

This site supports announcements, artwork, backgrounds and other remote content used by Verdant worlds.
